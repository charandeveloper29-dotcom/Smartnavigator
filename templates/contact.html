Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new place?
Click "+ Add Place" in the navbar. Search any place worldwide — we automatically fetch its real photo from Wikipedia.
Is Smart Navigator free to use?
Yes! Smart Navigator is completely free. Explore, save, and review places without any charges.
How does OTP verification work?
Enter your mobile number on the splash screen, receive a 6-digit OTP, and verify to create your account securely.
Can I suggest a place that's missing?
Absolutely! Use the "+ Add Place" feature to add any destination worldwide, or contact us with your suggestion.
How are nearby places calculated?
We use the Haversine formula to calculate exact distances between GPS coordinates. You can filter by 20, 50, 100, or 500 km radius.
How do I change my profile photo?
Go to Profile and hover over your avatar to see the camera icon. Click it to upload a photo from your device.
